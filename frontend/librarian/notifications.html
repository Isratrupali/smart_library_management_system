Notifications are delivered instantly to the student's dashboard notification panel.

HOW IT WORKS

This uses the Observer Pattern: the librarian acts as the Publisher (Subject), and each student is a Subscriber (Observer). Sending a notification calls POST /api/librarian/notify which writes a row to the notifications table. The student sees it next time they check their dashboard.
